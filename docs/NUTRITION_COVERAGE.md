# Release Step 3 — traceable nutrition coverage

Updated 2026-10-25. Active development pair: candidate `0.1.0-rc.5`, index
`0.1.0-dev.4`. This is an engineering/data-coverage improvement, not scientific
approval, a measured nutrition label, allergy certification or a validated recipe.

## What changed

- Optional total `sugar_g` per 100 g in schema 1.1. Sugar follows the existing
  exact-form weighted calculation: sum(ingredient grams × value per 100 g / 100).
  The sum is unknown if any positive-mass ingredient lacks that nutrient. Missing
  values and USDA assumed-zero derivations never become numeric zero. Total sugar
  is not added sugar; carbohydrate is never substituted for sugar.
- USDA sugar comes from nutrient 2000, **Sugars, Total**, unit G, exact existing
  food IDs in the original SR Legacy archive. Derivation, source row, food identity,
  publication date and archive hash are retained in `nutrition_step3_sources.json`.
  Some usable USDA entries are imputed rather than analytical; this is visible in
  the audit. Fourteen of eighteen USDA profiles have numeric sugar; the new label
  makes fifteen of nineteen profiles overall.
- Chlorella uses the **complete Algomed Chlorella Pulver supplier label**: per
  100 g, protein 51.9 g, energy 377 kcal, carbohydrate 9.2 g, fat 9.3 g, sugar 0.6 g.
  The manufacturer product page was checked on 2026-10-25. These rounded label
  values are not general values for every chlorella powder.
- The old laboratory sample-A profile (protein 40.1 g, energy unknown, lipid
  measured as FAME) remains in rc.4 and in the source audit. None of its individual
  values are carried into the new label profile. No 4/4/9 calorie inference is used.
- New source `SRC-ALGOMED-POWDER-2026` and separate material `MAT-CHL-ALGOMED`;
  `IF-CHL-PWD` is explicitly named and linked to this reference in rc.5. This is a
  versioned reference change, not a claim that old sensory studies tested Algomed.
- Form preflight, reports and JSON retain the named reference and source records.
  Preflight identifies missing ingredient composition and gives numeric margins
  when available. Manufacturer-reported naturally occurring sulfites are disclosed;
  no free-from relationship was invented.
- The 23 RAG evidence notes are unchanged. A new immutable index pairs them with
  the new dataset hash. Old dataset/index pairs remain usable and unchanged.
  Working CSVs retain repository LF line endings; frozen generated bytes/hashes
  are preserved, so record values match even when line endings differ.
- Schema 1.0 CSVs are accepted only with their exact legacy header; sugar defaults
  to unknown. Legacy audit shapes are preserved so old review hashes still verify.
  A manifest must declare the schema actually represented by its CSV header.

## Supported versus unknown

Vanilla and cocoa-spice recipes have protein, energy and total sugar coverage for
spirulina, chlorella and blend. The usual ingredient proportions still contain
dates and therefore sugar: a zero-sugar request correctly fails. The finite recipe
grid cannot satisfy arbitrary protein or calorie targets.

Raw orange peel has no total sugar in this source. Citrus recipes therefore block
an explicit sugar target. They can run with that optional target blank, while
reporting sugar unknown. Coconut oil, salt and soy isolate assumed-zero sugar
entries also remain unknown; they are not used in the original nine-point grid
(`exact-form-grid-v1`). The live screen is `protein-base-grid-v2`; its ranges
are recorded separately and do not replace the v1 numbers below.

Fibre/iron and total omega-3 are not complete for every recipe. No missing field
was invented to make the app produce an apparently complete report.

| Energy | 154.017–159.201 kcal |
| Total sugar | 7.508205–10.419345 g |

Requested minimum protein 12 g, maximum energy 426 kcal, maximum sugar 0 g blocks
before job creation. Messages advise protein minimum at most 5.17 g and sugar
maximum at least 7.51 g, noting that all targets must pass together. With protein
5.17 g, energy 200 kcal, sugar 10 g, exactly S25-1 passes: 5.17797 g protein,
159.201 kcal, 7.508205 g total sugar. Recipe: dates 11.25 g, reference chlorella
1.35 g, oats 31.95 g, vanilla extract 0.45 g. It is not a sensory-validated bar.

Primary sources:

- [USDA original SR Legacy archive](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov/fdc-datasets/FoodData_Central_sr_legacy_food_csv_2018-04.zip)
- [Algomed manufacturer product label](https://algomed.de/products/algomed-chlorella-pulver)

Manufacturer factual extraction:
`data/extractions/algomed_chlorella_label_2026-10-25.json` (full page/images not
redistributed). USDA local archive SHA-256:
`b80817294b8850530aaedf2e515c02593b1824f763a0ff356e5c2081643e6fd0`.
The source audit records that hash, label-extraction hash and frozen nutrition CSV
hash. No data was promoted to approved.

From the repository root, choose **new paths and versions**; existing artifacts are
never overwritten:

```sh
.venv/bin/python tools/extend_nutrition_dataset.py \
  --output var/nutrition-new/reviewed --audit var/nutrition-new/sources.json
.venv/bin/python tools/publish_dataset.py candidate NEW_VERSION \
  --input var/nutrition-new/reviewed
HF_HUB_OFFLINE=1 .venv/bin/python tools/rag.py build \
  --dataset data/candidates/NEW_VERSION --version NEW_INDEX_VERSION --development
```

The extension is based explicitly on preserved rc.4. Its optional
`--sync-working-copy` refuses to overwrite working CSVs if any differ from that
base; it has already been applied here. For further updates, create a reviewed
extension from the appropriate next base; do not reuse this historical migration
to downgrade the current working copy.

Current audit artifacts: `output/review/nutrition_step3_sources.json`,
`rc5_scoped_nutrition_audit.json`, `rc5_dev4_retrieval_evaluation.json`.
The independent scoped USDA audit checks five core recipe ingredients (including
sugar). Changed protein, changed sugar, wrong basis and duplicate records are
deliberately tested and rejected. The source extension records all eighteen USDA
sugar decisions, separately from the supplier profile.

## Activation

Restart the backend and rebuild/relaunch Flutter together, normally with
`python3 tools/dev.py`. Explicit environment overrides of dataset/index paths must
be changed together or removed. Historical saved reports retain their original
data; rerun an experiment to use rc.5. Existing services are not silently replaced.
