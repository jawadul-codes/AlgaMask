# Ingredient card assets

Six transparent PNG cutouts in `apps/flutter/assets/ingredients/` were generated with the built-in image tool for the experiment builder.

Prompt template used for each asset:

> Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent ingredient cutout for a small selection card in a premium scientific food formulation web app. Subject: {subject}. Composition: centered, isolated, compact arrangement occupying the middle 75% of a square canvas, slight three-quarter viewpoint, soft studio light, realistic tactile detail, subtle grounded shadow. Consistent visual style across a set of six cards. Background: genuinely transparent alpha. Constraints: no text, no label, no logo, no container, no utensils, no human, no extra ingredients, no white background, no watermark.

Subjects:

- `spirulina.png`: a single neat mound of deep teal-green spirulina powder
- `chlorella.png`: a single neat mound of fresh olive-green chlorella powder
- `blend.png`: two small adjacent mounds of deep teal spirulina powder and olive-green chlorella powder
- `cocoa-spice.png`: a few dark chocolate squares, one cinnamon stick, and a tiny dusting of cocoa powder
- `vanilla.png`: one cream-colored vanilla flower and two dark vanilla pods
- `citrus.png`: one orange wedge, one lime wedge, and a small curled strip of citrus peel
